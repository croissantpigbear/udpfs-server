UDPFS Server for Mac
====================

Nothing to install: Python is included (Intel and Apple silicon).

1. Drag "UDPFS Server" into your Applications folder.

2. First open: the app is not signed, so macOS refuses it once.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and press
   "Open Anyway" next to UDPFS Server. (On older macOS: right-click the app
   and choose Open.)
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/UDPFS Server.app"

3. Pick your OPL folder - the one with DVD, CD, ART, CFG and VMC inside
   (for example /Volumes/MyDrive/PS2) - and press Start.

4. The first time you press Start, macOS asks to let UDPFS Server find
   devices on your local network: say Allow (without it the PS2 never
   finds the server). If it asks to accept incoming connections or read a
   removable volume, say Allow too. If your Mac's firewall is on, press "Allow through firewall" once
   and enter your password.

OPL Library (or the "OPL Library..." button): your games in one list.
Rename a game, edit its info by hand, fetch info from the PS2 OPL CFG
Database, and compress ISOs to ZSO. Keep OPL Library next to UDPFS Server
(both in Applications): it uses UDPFS Server's Python. Game settings and
memory cards are never touched; changed CFG files are backed up to
CFG/info-backup.
Fetch info also fills what that database lacks (publisher, genre,
description...) from the libretro database. Check Redump reads each game
and compares its checksum with Redump's list of good dumps: verified, not
a Redump dump (patched, trimmed or damaged), or not in Redump. CD games
can only be size-checked (Redump keeps CDs as .bin).
Click games to tick them (Shift-click for a range, Select all or Cmd+A
for every game shown); Fetch info and Compress use the ticked games, or
all games when none are ticked.

RetroAchievements: the window also runs xeRAbora. It finds xerabora in
Downloads or on the Desktop, or press Download in the RetroAchievements
box. With "Start xeRAbora with the server" ticked, Start runs both.

The dot turns green when the PS2 talks to the server. Keep the app open
while you play. "Open at login" starts it minimized and already serving.

If the PS2 never finds the server: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Local Network, and switch on UDPFS Server. (After replacing the app with a
new version, macOS may ask again.)
