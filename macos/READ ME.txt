UDPFS Server for Mac
====================

Nothing to install: Python is included (Intel and Apple silicon).

1. Drag "UDPFS Server" into your Applications folder.

2. First open: the app is not signed, so macOS refuses it once.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and press
   "Open Anyway" next to UDPFS Server. (On older macOS: right-click the app
   and choose Open.)
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/UDPFS Server.app"

3. Pick your OPL folder - the one with DVD, CD, ART, CFG and VMC inside
   (for example /Volumes/MyDrive/PS2) - and press Start.

4. If macOS asks to let UDPFS Server or Python find devices on your local
   network, accept incoming connections, or read a removable volume, say
   Allow. If your Mac's firewall is on, press "Allow through firewall" once
   and enter your password.

The dot turns green when the PS2 talks to the server. Keep the app open
while you play. "Open at login" starts it minimized and already serving.

If the PS2 never finds the server: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Local Network, and switch on UDPFS Server (or Python).
