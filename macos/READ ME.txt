UDPFS Server for Mac
====================

1. Install Python 3 from python.org (https://www.python.org/downloads/macos/).
   Its installer includes the window toolkit the app needs.
   (Homebrew works too: brew install python python-tk)

2. Drag "UDPFS Server" into your Applications folder.

3. First open: the app is not signed, so macOS will refuse it once.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and press
   "Open Anyway" next to UDPFS Server. (On older macOS: right-click the app
   and choose Open.)
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/UDPFS Server.app"

4. Pick your OPL folder - the one with DVD, CD, ART, CFG and VMC inside
   (for example /Volumes/MyDrive/PS2) - and press Start.

5. If macOS asks to let UDPFS Server or Python find devices on your local
   network, or to accept incoming connections, say Allow. If your Mac's
   firewall is on, press "Allow through firewall" once and enter your
   password.

The dot turns green when the PS2 talks to the server. Keep the app open
while you play. "Open at login" starts it minimized and already serving.

Drives formatted exFAT or FAT32 on a Mac work as they are. The PS2 also
writes to this folder (configs, VMCs), so the folder must not be
read-only.

If the PS2 never finds the server: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Local Network, and switch on UDPFS Server (or Python).
